Paid Ephemeris Gateway

A deterministic apparent tropical ephemeris for one exact UTC instant: ten bodies with sign, degree, and motion; lunar phase; major aspects with applying or separating phase; provenance; and audited error bounds. Zero-dependency, computed from truncated Meeus series and JPL approximate elements, and independently audited against astronomy-engine (VSOP87/ELP) across its valid range of 1800–2050.

Payment is x402 v2, settled in USDC on Base. There is no account, no API key, and no signup — an agent pays per call and receives the response. The endpoint's own 402 Payment Required challenge is authoritative for its current asset, payee, and price; the figures below are a convenience, and where they disagree with the live challenge, the challenge is right.
