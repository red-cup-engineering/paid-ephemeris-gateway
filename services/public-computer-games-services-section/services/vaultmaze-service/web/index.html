Click or press Enter on a flickering room to open its gate. Arrow keys move between rooms.

Map legend

  • Full light — reviewed, well within retention
  • Frontier — walkable now: gate it, or it's fading
  • Ash — territory you've seen, currently out of reach
  • Fog — not yet discovered

Territory revealed is permanent. Fog only ever hides the path back to a room, never the proof you were there.
