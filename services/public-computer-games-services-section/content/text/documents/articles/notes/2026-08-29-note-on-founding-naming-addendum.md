Recovered 2026-08-29 from the founding session transcript (session 47cac153, 2026-08-19).
Addendum to [Naming the Three Houses](2026-08-19-note-on-naming-the-three-houses.md) — the
candidates and closing hedge below surfaced in the session's own working-through of the naming
problem but never made it into that note. Nothing here revisits the DECIDED names (Cowrie ·
Ashlar · Ilinx); this is the discarded working material, preserved for the record.

## Consumer slot (Cowrie's competitors)

**Hermit Crab** — a hermit crab carrying its shell mirrors games living inside a shared shell or
cartridge. Arguably the best shell-metaphor of the entire batch, including Cowrie itself; dropped
silently, with no rejection reason recorded anywhere in the session.

## Science slot (Ilinx's competitors)

- **Ludeme** — rejected: collides with Cameron Browne's Ludii project.
- **Senet, Patolli, Oware, Rundle** — historical/academic game names, dropped in shortlisting
  before the field narrowed to Ilinx / Paidia / Astragal / Alea.

## Engineering slot (Ashlar's competitors)

- **Flume** — "engineered water channel driving machinery."
- **Sluice** — "ties nicely to gating/contracts."

Both dropped silently while Kiln, Bellows, Weir, Dovetail, Foundry, and Keystone made the
published note's runner-up list.

## The closing aesthetic hedge

The session's own closing line, never carried into the note: "Cowrie, Drystone, and Ilinx form a
coastal thread — shell, wall, whirlpool — ... though the otter option is worth noting too." Drystone
was superseded by the owner's Ashlar substitution before publication; the otter aside (Pebble
Otter, the naming note's other runner-up for the consumer slot) was never resolved either way.
