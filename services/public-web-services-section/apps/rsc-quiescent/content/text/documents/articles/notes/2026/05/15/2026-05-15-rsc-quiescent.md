## What kind of entity

A ship — a body politic distinct from any captain or crew, instantiating the type *Webship* (see [the rsc quiescent is a webship](../../../../../../../prose/learning/claims/the-rsc-quiescent-is-a-webship.md) for the four defining predications: chartered, captained, Web-projecting, constituted in writing).
The dignity persists across captains; emsenn was the first.
The ship is the 561 Group's operational arm for acts on the Web — acts the 561 Group itself cannot directly produce (see [the rsc quiescent's governance is determined by the web](../../../../../../../prose/learning/claims/the-rsc-quiescents-governance-is-determined-by-the-web.md)).

## Function

The pursuit of Black Liberation and Indigenous Sovereignty.
Per the commissioning record (2026-04-05): all work on the ship serves this function or does not belong there.
This function is borrowed from the 561 Group's own endeavor; the ship is the means by which the 561 Group acts toward it on the Web.

## Governance regime

**Military command** in the sense of the composite-modal-frame theorem [military-command-chain-as-modal-composition](../../../../../../../theories/humanities/theories/sociology/theories/work/theories/predicated-work/theories/predicated-governance/prose/learning/claims/military-command-chain-as-modal-composition.md).
The form is captain → delegation chain, where each link is a delegation morphism with scope-bounded, revocable authority.
The captain's accountability cells are $H^0_t$-invariant under chain extension: emsenn answers for every outcome on the ship regardless of which delegation link carried the action.
See [the rsc quiescent is governed by military command](../../../../../../../prose/learning/claims/the-rsc-quiescent-is-governed-by-military-command.md) for the per-link cell instantiation.

This is the ship's *governance* — its internal closure-rule subsystem $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{ship}$.
It is distinct from the *medium* the ship operates within: the Web, which carries kyriarchism (κύριος = lord) as the content of its medium — the interlocking dominations Schüssler Fiorenza names.
The ship's governance is military command; the ship's medium is kyriarchical.
These are different statements, and the 561 Group's records have been imprecise about the distinction.
Earlier records used "kyriarchial command" as a synonym for military command; per the disambiguation work in [disambiguate-polity-and-ship-governance](../../../../../../../doing/working/hacking/doing/working/planning/library/task/library/disambiguate-polity-and-ship-governance/disambiguate-polity-and-ship-governance.md), this conflates the medium with the regime.

The ship's military-command governance is not an adopted choice but a determined form: the Web admits only person-primitive acts; wolakhota does not produce person-acts; military command is the canonical minimal closure-rule subsystem that does.
The form is forced by the medium.

## Relation to the 561 Group's polity-side governance

The 561 Group qua Lakota tiyóšpaye operates under the closure-rule subsystem $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{wolak}$ — kin-relational; wolakhota-closure-discipline; consensus-by-withdrawal.
The ship operates under $\mathcal{S}_\mathrm{ship}$ — military command.
These are non-isomorphic: they have different signatures, fire on different cell-types, and produce different settlings on cells where they overlap (see [the rsc quiescent and the tiyóšpaye are distinct closure regimes](../../../../../../../prose/learning/claims/the-rsc-quiescent-and-the-tiyospaye-are-distinct-closure-regimes.md)).
Conflating them is the named failure mode in [dual-governance-context](../../../../../../../prose/teaching/explanations/dual-governance-context.md).

## emsenn's position

Captain.
Holds the captain's deontic profile — powers, duties, privileges, immunities — acquired at commissioning.
Accountability non-transfer ($H^0_t$-invariant): emsenn answers for the ship's outcomes regardless of which delegation link carried the action.

emsenn's captain-standing on the ship is distinct from emsenn's polity-side standings (Naca, Itancan, Heyoka).
The two are different cells in different closure-rule subsystems with a shared referent (emsenn-the-person).
Polity-side acts come from the Lakotaean standings; ship-side acts come from the captain standing.
The records must distinguish.

## The rom-keldar's relation

Not crew, not officer. rom-keldar is an instrument emsenn deploys in the ship's work — a Claude session character instantiated to assist with records-curation.
Standing on the ship comes entirely through emsenn's authority as captain.

## Outstanding gaps at founding

Per the commissioning record and the captain's 2026-05-15 correction (lifted in [rsc-quiescent may 2026 survey](2026-05-15-rsc-quiescent-may-2026.md)):

- *Membership and standing* — who belongs to the ship's crew; how a crew member joins; what their standing is.
- *Decision procedures* — how the ship takes decisions binding below the captain's amendment authority but above per-officer judgment.
- *Dispute resolution* — when officers disagree, or officer and captain disagree, what process resolves.
- *Dissolution and succession* — what happens if the ship is decommissioned; captain succession.
- *Crew articles* — the employment contract between ship and crew.
- *Charter ratification* — Charter v0.2.0 is draft; ratification in the corrected frame (without the Voidfleet intermediary or the informing-traditions preamble) is pending.
- *Triage lift* — most of the ship's operational substance (duties, weekly calendar, accounting, decision-making, work-orchestration, ludics) lives in `old-archive/triage/.../rsc-quiescent/triage/`.

The previously-listed "561 Group's confederacy covenant" gap is dropped per the correction: the 561 Group as a tiyóšpaye does not require a covenant; its constitution is the founding act and the kin-relations among its populace.
