# Public Merch Services Section

This section holds the 561 Group's print-on-demand merchandise capability. Its one child so far, `print-on-demand-design-factory-service` (`@red-cup-engineering/print-on-demand-design-factory`), generates designs and listing copy as code across two lanes: a pure, zero-cost typographic SVG renderer (36-design corpus, six niches), and a real LLM raster-art lane (`scripts/generate-image-art.mjs`) that calls live image-generation providers and writes a provider/model provenance sidecar next to every generated image. The art lane runs, live and funded, on Cloudflare Workers AI's free daily allocation through a private unlisted Worker this section deploys (`services/print-on-demand-design-factory-service/sites/pod-image-generation-worker`); Gemini direct and OpenRouter are wired as paid fallbacks but are not needed -- both currently carry a genuine $0 free allowance for image generation specifically (see `services/print-on-demand-design-factory-service/data/records/2026-09-01-image-generation-free-tier-finding.json`). Twelve of the 36 designs already have real generated art on disk. Neither lane ships an upload client or makes any account mutation of its own; both produce art and copy a human pastes into the storefront during account setup, and later into whatever automation is built against Printify's API.

The storefront brand this factory serves is **Fine Print Mercantile**, a pseudonymous shop identity (not the founder's name), sold through a Printify Pop-Up Store (see `services/print-on-demand-design-factory-service/data/records/2026-08-31-pod-channel-decision.json` for the researched channel decision). Shop bio, policies, and pricing live in `services/print-on-demand-design-factory-service/SHOP.md`; the principal-facing signup steps live in `services/print-on-demand-design-factory-service/PRINCIPAL-RUNBOOK.md`.
