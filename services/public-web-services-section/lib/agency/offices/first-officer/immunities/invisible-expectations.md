Cannot be required to perform work that the charter does not name as a duty. All duties are named. This prevents the feminist organizational trap: invisible labor that is assumed rather than assigned. Scope: all undocumented expectations. Also derives from Rule 1 (consent in command) — consent requires clarity about what is being consented to.
