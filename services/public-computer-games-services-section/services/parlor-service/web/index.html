The Parlor
Practice scorekeeping only, on every game here. Nothing is staked, nothing is wagered, no currency of any kind enters or leaves. Every decision that has a real probability or expected value behind it shows the computed number, live, next to the decision — not behind a click, never asserted without the arithmetic that produced it.

One shared kernel (@red-cup-engineering/parlor-kernel), games as data plus a thin face. Every game is daily-seeded — today's dice are the same for everyone who plays them today — and every game also offers a fresh random game any time.

  • Pig

    Hold-or-roll, first to 100. The computed bust probability and expected value of rolling again sit next to every decision. Bot opponent plays the exact published math.

  • Farkle

    Dice-set scoring, hot dice, and a real chance to bust the whole turn. Same computed-odds treatment; same kind of bot opponent.

  • Yacht

    Five dice, three rolls, 12 standard categories. Every category shows its exact score-if-you-commit-now and its exact expected value if you reroll once more, recomputed live after every roll and every hold.

  • Chutes & Ladders

    The classic 100-square race as a board-as-graph. 1-4 players, any mix of humans and bots — the bot is a die, not an opponent, since there's no strategy in this game. The exact probability distribution of finish turns updates live as the race runs.

  • Fair Spin

    A 3-reel practice slot that inverts the genre: computed RTP, per-symbol hit odds, and expected loss per 100 spins are shown permanently on the machine face, never behind a click. Practice bankroll only.

Every rule and every odds figure on this shelf is computed by @red-cup-engineering/parlor-kernel, not hand-typed.
