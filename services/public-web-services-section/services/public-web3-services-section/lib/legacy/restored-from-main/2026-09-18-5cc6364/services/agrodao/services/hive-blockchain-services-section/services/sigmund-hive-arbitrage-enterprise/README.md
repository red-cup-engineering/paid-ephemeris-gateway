# Sigmund Hive Arbitrage Enterprise

This xiaowei turns current Hive/Hive-Engine price disagreement into bounded,
exact arbitrage proposals. It produces a reserve declaration and an exact plan
digest. AGRODAO's treasury steward admits or refuses that declaration against a
fresh fleet inventory; Aagaard alone may execute an admitted envelope.

Its outputs are strategy and authorization requests, never transactions. It
must refuse negative or unquantized returns, stale market evidence, incomplete
legs, unbounded slippage, self-cross risk, missing settlement evidence, and any
use of a protected asset. It may not provide pool liquidity or treat the
editorial role of `sigmund-vexler` as trading authority.
