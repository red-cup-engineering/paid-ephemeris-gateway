Operates Jordan Farmstead in Farr Valley, four-generation family dairy on 18 ha.
The farmstead is one of the two valley dairies cited in [`farr-river`](../places/farr-river.md) for chronic riparian-buffer compliance issues — a seeded slow-burn dispute that exercises the substrate's anomaly-handling on a longer timescale than the hydrant-23 case.

Ben is articulate at council meetings, generally sympathetic to enforcement, frustrated with what he considers shifting state-level standards.
