Cowrie Casino

casino.561.group — a real, monetized social casino: tickets-for-play (timed regeneration plus purchasable bundles) spend down into chips, the play currency, with a shared jackpot pool funded by a small rake on every chip wager and an escalating daily bonus. No real-money wagering or payouts, ever — every ticket and chip balance is a @red-cup-engineering/energy-bank double-entry ledger account, auditable live as an hledger journal. Two real games are on the floor: Blackjack (a seeded 6-deck shoe, @red-cup-engineering/card-table, dealer plays a published H17/S17 policy, a live per-decision House Math EV panel) and "Tideline Treasures" (the flagship spectacle game — a 5-reel/3-row/20-payline video slot on @red-cup-engineering/parlor-kernel's multi-payline grid math, wilds, scatters, a retriggerable free-spin bonus, and a progressive jackpot fed by the shared pool, rendered with @red-cup-engineering/qwzspace's procedural drawables/particles/celebration layer and scored with @red-cup-engineering/resonator's procedural audio). Every RTP/odds figure either game discloses is computed by exact enumeration or closed-form analysis, never asserted, in each game's own House Math panel. The floor is real-time and social, not just two solo games behind a lobby: a @red-cup-engineering/qwzspace room (src/floor/) tracks live presence (humans by WS session, @red-cup-engineering/cast named agent regulars by persona — a UTC-day-seeded rotation of the eight authored personas, actually playing real chip accounts through the identical BlackjackTable/SlotsTable modules a human's HTTP request calls, via a direct in-process Transport rather than a self-HTTP loopback), a live jackpot ticker, and tiered win broadcasts hooked additively onto both games' own settlement points.

Playable

This pass adds the realtime social floor (src/floor/) — a third, DIFFERENT replay contract from cartridgeWrapped's own (a @red-cup-engineering/qwzspace room, not a @red-cup-engineering/cartridge game), so it changes nothing about that field: the floor room's own self-audit is honestly disabled (selfAuditEvery: 0, see src/floor/world.mts's header) because its jackpot-balance field is deliberately live, externally-owned CasinoEconomy state, not a pure fold of the room's own intent log — the identical, already-precedented scope note @red-cup-engineering/qwzspace/lobby documents for its own live member-count reads. status is playable for the floor too: it is real and tested (real cast personas playing real chip accounts through the same BlackjackTable/SlotsTable modules a human's HTTP request calls), and the same promotion pass that cleared both games — this pass — applies to it as well.
