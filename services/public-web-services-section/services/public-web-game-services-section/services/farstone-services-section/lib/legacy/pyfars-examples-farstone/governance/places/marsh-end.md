# Marsh-End

The town's northeast neighborhood, where Main Street's grid loosens into rural lots before giving way to [`the-marsh`](the-marsh.md).
Low-density residential — single-family homes on quarter-acre to two-acre lots, older houses near the original road and post-war infill closer to the marsh.
About 600 residents.

Marsh-end's seaward edge is the Marsh boundary; the boundary is contested in places (the Marsh expands and contracts with sea-level events), and a parcels-near-marsh map is updated after major coastal storms.

Marsh-end falls within the town's water-distribution and dispatch coverage; it is just barely within the volunteer fire department's 8-minute response radius from the downtown station.
