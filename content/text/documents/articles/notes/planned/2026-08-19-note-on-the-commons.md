# The Commons

A massively-cooperative persistent shared world. Every other game concept in the estate — idle economies, obstruction-based costing, daily-seeded risk, shared boards, ecological simulation — is groundwork toward this one destination, approached from a different side. It isn't itself a specified game; it's the thing the specified games are triangulating toward.

The concept is genuinely open-ended — the owner has described it as "too wild to express" in its current form. There's no first step to name here that isn't just one of the other games: each of the shared-board and cooperative-world games elsewhere in this notes directory is itself the closest thing this vision has to a first step.
