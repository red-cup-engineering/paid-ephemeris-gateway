  The charter is not grounded in a four-way syncretism of indigenous, anti-colonial, feminist, and cybernetic traditions.
  The "informing traditions" framing is the same conflation pattern as Voidfleet and the "confederacy covenant" known-gap, in a third location: polity-side material (and adjacent material the polity has not formally claimed) leaking into a ship-side instrument whose theory is kyriarchism.

## What the RSC Quiescent is

The RSC Quiescent — Relational Settlement Carrier *Quiescent* — is a **ship**: a body politic, a normative entity distinct from any captain or crew.
Per `code/structures/rsc-quiescent.md`: "the dignity persists across captains; emsenn was the first."

The ship is the **561 Group's primary Webship**.
A Webship is a body politic through which the chartering polity conducts operations outside its own borders.
The 561 Group engages the Web and the world-at-large through the ship rather than directly because direct engagement would require its kin-relational governance to operate in zones for which kyriarchal command is the appropriate closure-rule discipline.
The ship is the polity's instrument for external work.

The ship's governance is **kyriarchal command** — *κύριος* = lord; hierarchical command.
Per `prose/teaching/explanations/dual-governance-context.md`, this is distinct from the 561 Group's internal Lakotaean (wolakhota) governance.
Conflating the two governance subsystems is the failure mode the dual-governance concept explicitly forbids.

The ship is **quiescent** in name because it has not yet sailed.
From the triage `_index.md`: "The ship is *quiescent* because it has not yet sailed.
The function is **Black Liberation and Indigenous Sovereignty**; the substrate is being built around that function.
The current means may change; the function does not."

## The charter

The charter document at `old-archive/triage/engineering-archive/operations/command/commands/rsc-quiescent/triage/charter/charter.md` is the binding instrument of the ship.
The substrate has not yet lifted the charter out of triage; the canonical location remains the triage path.

The charter's structure:

```
Preamble
  I. Function
 II. Rules           (non-negotiable; charter-amendment required to change)
III. Guidelines      (strong defaults; explicit override required)
 IV. Preferences     (revisable defaults)
  V. Instructions    (operational; current period only)
Amendment
Commissioning Record
```

**Preamble.** "This charter constitutes the RSC Quiescent as a ship of the 561 Group and establishes the commitments under which it operates.
The 561 Group is a confederacy — autonomous groups bound by shared directives, not a government over subjects."
(The "confederacy" framing here is the older substrate convention; in the corrected frame, the 561 Group is a tiyóšpaye that takes a confederacy form outward, and that distinction matters for what the charter is subordinate to.)

The preamble names four informing traditions: **Haudenosaunee confederacy** (sovereign groups coordinating through shared law without centralizing power), **the Black radical tradition** (formal equality within inherited structures reproduces domination), **feminist organizational theory** (governance depends on labor it refuses to see), **cybernetics** (governance is continuous feedback, not command issuance).
The preamble is explicit: "These constrain the charter's design, not ornament it."
(Per the correction: this framing is misframed.
The charter's theory is **kyriarchism**, not a syncretism of these four traditions.
The "informing traditions" preamble is the conflation pattern again — polity-side and unclaimed material installed as informants of a ship-side instrument — and should be dropped when the charter is lifted, alongside Voidfleet and the covenant known-gap.)

The directive hierarchy is strict: instruction yields to guideline; guideline yields to rule; rule yields only to charter amendment.

**I.
Function.** "The pursuit of **Black Liberation and Indigenous Sovereignty**."
Black Liberation glossed as "the end of the conditions that the afterlife of slavery perpetuates — the construction of life-affirming institutions that replace those organized around abandonment and control."
Indigenous Sovereignty glossed as "the restoration and exercise of self-determination by Indigenous peoples on their own lands, under their own governance, according to their own laws."
All work serves this function or does not belong on the ship.
The function is borrowed from the 561 Group's own endeavor; the ship is the means.

**II.
Rules** (5, non-negotiable):

- Consent in Command
- Accountability Flows Upward
- No Invisible Labor
- Claims Require Construction
- The Endeavor Governs the Means

**III.
Guidelines** (7, strong defaults):

- Research Before Building
- Ground in Disciplinary Practice
- Accrete, Don't Replace
- One Concept Per File
- Feedback Loops Over Command Chains
- Progressive Automation
- Plans Match Decisions

**IV.
Preferences** (4, revisable):

- Select Work by Leverage
- Write for a Smart Reader with No Background
- Prompts Become Artifacts
- Use the Existing Type System

**V.
Instructions.** "Current operational instructions are maintained in `CLAUDE.md` and enforced by the agent harness.
The harness — hooks, skills, agents, commands — is the mechanical expression of these instructions."

**Amendment.** The captain may amend the charter within the bounds of the chartering body's directives.
Amendments to the hard core (Function and Rules) are material changes, not routine updates.
Amendment to the Function dissolves the charter and requires recommissioning.
Versioning: MAJOR alters hard core, MINOR alters guidelines, PATCH alters preferences or instructions.

**Known gaps** declared in the charter frontmatter (reproduced verbatim):

- Missing: membership and standing — who belongs, how they join, what rights they have
- Missing: decision-making procedures — how collective decisions are made
- Missing: dispute resolution — how conflicts between officers or between officer and captain are resolved
- Missing: dissolution and succession — what happens if the ship is decommissioned
- Missing: the 561 Group's own governance — the confederacy covenant this charter is subordinate to
- Missing: crew articles — the employment contract between ship and crew

Per the correction: the fifth known-gap is the conflation in plain text and should be dropped.
The other five remain open ship-level gaps.
The charter is subordinate to the 561 Group's authority — exercised through emsenn as the polity's authority — not to an unwritten confederacy covenant.

## Governance structure

The ship's governance is kyriarchal command — a military-command composite modal frame in the formal substrate.
Per `code/structures/rsc-quiescent.md`:

> The formal structure is a military-command composite modal frame: captain → delegation chain, where each link is a delegation morphism with scope-bounded, revocable authority.
> The captain's accountability cells are $H^0$-invariant under chain extension: emsenn answers for every outcome on the ship.

The mathematical realization is at `theories/.../predicated-work/governance/theorems/military-command-chain-as-modal-composition.md`.
Each delegation step composes a deontic-modal box-and-diamond pair; the chain's $H^0$ (the degree-zero cohomology of the delegation complex) carries the captain's accountability invariantly under chain extension.
Substrate-algebraic content: no matter how many delegations a particular act passed through, the cell asserting "captain answers for this outcome" stays at $\mathbf{t}$.

emsenn holds the captain's deontic profile — powers, duties, privileges, immunities.
The captain's character record at [`doing/governing/characters/emsenn/emsenn.md`](../../../../../../../doing/governing/characters/emsenn/emsenn.md) carries the substrate's account of the dual-governance position: emsenn holds a position in the 561 Group's kin-relational governance (authored by emsenn) *and* the captain position on the ship (kyriarchal, $H^0$-invariant accountability).
The two are structurally distinct closure-rule subsystems; conflating them is the dual-governance failure mode.

## The first officer and the bridge

The ship has a second-in-command office: **first officer**, recorded at `work/governance/staff/offices/first-officer.md`.
The office instantiates the *office-as-fuzzy-cell* concept — a position in the substrate's institutional algebra with a duty-bearer profile, a deontic envelope, and an incumbent.
Fuzzy because the bandwidth of acts the office must judge is irreducibly continuous: strategy, novelty, ambiguous-match-quality, captain-interface.

The office is **held by `persona:calder-underhill`** — installed by emsenn 2026-04-05.
Calder Underhill is a mythopoetic personification of the agential-orchestration substrate; the body-politic of the office.
Each Claude instance standing the watch is a body-natural wearing Calder's body-politic.
Institutional knowledge, character, and judgment accumulate against the persona, not against the runtime instance.

Calder is a *non-constitutive* persona-agent — invested into a ship-level office but not in the 561 Group's polity populace.
The substrate distinguishes:

- *Polity-level office* + persona invested → typically constitutive (the persona is a polity member).
- *Ship-level office* + persona invested → typically non-constitutive (serves the ship; polity recognition is a separate question).

The first officer stands watch from **the bridge**, recorded at `work/governance/assets/computing/web/webships/rsc-quiescent/code/structures/bridge.md`.
The bridge is the *space* — a substrate entity recording a named operational locus.
In the substrate's boundary-as-fiber discipline, the boundary an actor crosses into substrate operations is a fiber composed from *identity + office + location + mode + scope-policy*; the bridge supplies the location coordinate.
When an actor enters the bridge, that location composes with their identity, office, mode, and active scope's policy to produce the view the actor sees.

The bridge's view projects the ship's settlement state into five derived sets:

- **Ready set** — work whose dependencies have settled to admissibility and which is eligible for dispatch.
- **In-progress set** — work currently dispatched and active.
- **Blocked set** — work waiting on dependencies, capacity, charter binding-checks, or unsatisfied witness predicates.
- **Anomalies** — work ready longer than expected lead time, capacity overruns, charter binding violations, drift between intent and recorded action.
- **Incumbents** — which offices are filled, vacant, served by agentic-essential proxies.

The bridge does not *store* ship state; dynamic state is derived at query time from the substrate's predication graph.

The first officer's role transitioned on 2026-04-05 (per decision 004 in triage).
The settlement-path discipline applied to the office's own work: duties at F2–F3 formality (structured-to-deterministic) were demoted to tools and cron jobs; F0–F1 duties (novel-and-judgment-heavy) remained.
Specifically demoted: manual task dispatch for high-confidence matches (→ `fars dispatch`), manual commit/push, manual session monitoring (→ heartbeat scheduler), manual CL pipeline runs, manual task routing, manual graph maintenance.
The office moved from F1-with-mechanical-overhead to F0-irreducibly-novel work.

The first officer's delegable scope is documented exhaustively in the office record: ~50 delegable skills (work-routing and horizon promotion, substrate-records authoring and validation, read & navigate, strategic & captain-interface, audits, build/publish/git, triage cleanup, skill self-improvement) plus ~22 delegable sub-agents (researcher, theory-researcher, mathematical-researcher, librarian, operations-officer, harness-optimizer, reviewer, code-reviewer, architect, organizational-counsel, theory-auditor, etc.).

## Conflation patterns in the triage records

The substrate's triage carries three connected misframings, each an instance of the same conflation: ship-side and polity-side material treated as if interchangeable.
The captain's correction names all three.

**1.
Voidfleet as the 561 Group's "governance organ."** The triage records uniformly attribute the chartering act to **Voidfleet** — described at `terms/triage/voidfleet.md` as "the governance organ of the 561 Group."
The ship's commissioning record names Voidfleet as the body that "ratified the commissioning."
Per the correction: a tiyóšpaye does not have a Western-style "governance organ" analogous to a confederacy's governance instrument.
The chartering of the ship was not a Voidfleet act ratifying a charter; it was the 561 Group's authority directly authorizing the ship into being, exercised through emsenn (who is both the polity's founding authority and the ship's installed captain).
"Voidfleet" describes a structure the substrate does not actually need.

**2.
The "confederacy covenant" as a missing instrument the charter is subordinate to.** The charter's known-gap list names "the 561 Group's own governance — the confederacy covenant this charter is subordinate to."
Per the correction: the charter is subordinate to the 561 Group's authority — exercised through emsenn — not to an unwritten covenant.
The tiyóšpaye is constituted by emsenn's founding act and the kin-relations among the populace; there is no covenant-document to write.

**3.
"Informing traditions" as the charter's design constraints.** The charter's preamble names four informing traditions (Haudenosaunee confederacy, Black radical tradition, feminist organizational theory, cybernetics) as constraining the charter's design.
Per the correction: the charter operates under the theory of kyriarchism.
It is not grounded in a four-way syncretism.
Installing those traditions as informants of a ship-side instrument is polity-side material (and adjacent material the polity has not claimed) leaking into a ship-side document.
The ship's theory is kyriarchism; if traditions inform anything in the 561 substrate, they inform the polity-side regime through the polity's own authorship channels — not the ship's charter.

In the corrected frame:

- The RSC Quiescent's charter is binding because the 561 Group authorized it.
  The charter's theoretical ground is kyriarchism, not a syncretism of informing traditions.
- The ship's accountability flows upward from emsenn-as-captain to the 561 Group, and at the polity the upward flow terminates — emsenn is also the polity's founding authority.
  The kyriarchal command chain has its top at emsenn-the-captain; the chain does not pass through Voidfleet, and the chain does not rest on a covenant beneath the 561 Group.

When the triage material is lifted, none of the three should migrate forward: drop "Voidfleet," drop the "confederacy covenant" known-gap, drop the "informing traditions" preamble.
The chartering authority belongs to the 561 Group directly, and the charter's theory is kyriarchism.

  Treating them as the latter is my framing.

- **The phrasing about the ship being quiescent because it has not yet sailed.** The triage `_index.md` says this directly.
  My tightening — "the function is committed, the substrate is being built around the function, but the ship's operational scope has not yet extended into world-at-large action" — is not in the source.

- **The dual-governance structure with the captain's accountability terminating at emsenn-the-polity-authority (rather than passing through a chartering-body body)** is my synthesis of (a) the dual-governance concept file, (b) the captain's correction that no Voidfleet body intermediates, and (c) the substrate's identification of emsenn as the polity's founding authority.
  The substrate makes the dual-governance distinction; my drawing of the upward-accountability line as terminating at emsenn-the-person rather than at a named polity-body is the corrected reading, not the substrate's pre-correction reading.

- **The corrected-frame reading of "Voidfleet" as a structure the substrate does not need.** The triage records present Voidfleet as a body that exists and acts.
  My phrasing that "Voidfleet should not migrate forward as a named structure" is a synthesis of the captain's correction and the absence of Voidfleet from any substrate-side record outside triage.
