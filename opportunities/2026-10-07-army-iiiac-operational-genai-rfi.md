# Army IIIAC Operational Generative AI & Workforce Enablement Suite — RFI capture

## Economic effect sought

Position Causeway Services for a possible follow-on firm-fixed-price acquisition covering a 12-month base plus four 12-month option periods. This RFI itself does not award a contract and does not reimburse response preparation.

## Requirement shape

The anticipated service is a role-tailored operational GenAI training and workforce-enablement suite for Fort Hood, Texas:

- Track 1: Generative AI for Mission Leaders — 4 cohorts/year, 50–75 students/cohort.
- Track 2: Applied Prompt Engineering for Mission Operations — 12 cohorts/year, 20–25 students/cohort.
- Track 3: Autonomous Agent Architecture & Mission Systems Integration — 4 cohorts/year, 15–20 students/cohort.
- Defense-specific curriculum and vignettes.
- Prompt-injection and data-leakage mitigation.
- Pre-approved/tested prompt libraries.
- Agent architecture and secure mission-application/API integration.
- Lab integration for IL5, IL6, and isolated/air-gapped environments.
- No personnel clearance or facility clearance is stated as required; instructors must be technically qualified and familiar with DoD Cloud Computing Security Requirements Guide constraints.

## Submission package requested

1. Searchable PDF capability statement.
2. Separate non-binding ROM in PDF or XLSX.
3. Company identity, address, website, UEI, CAGE, POC, contract vehicles, and active SAM confirmation.
4. Technical answers on curriculum adaptability, secure environment integration, instructor qualifications, risks/dependencies, and up to three similar contracts.
5. Direct answers to the Government questions, including whether the firm can perform at least 50% of contract-performance cost as prime.
6. Email to both listed contracting contacts using the required subject line.

## Current qualification

### Positive fit

- Agent architecture, multi-step tool use, connector integration, evaluation, provenance, permissions, prompt-injection resistance, and fail-closed operation are directly aligned with existing 561 Group engineering/research work.
- The three-track structure maps naturally onto leader, operator, and technical-builder curricula.
- No clearance requirement lowers one major gate.
- The RFI is market research, so a useful response can shape the later acquisition without creating an offer or contract.

### Hard gates before any external response

Do not submit until each item is truthfully bound:

- exact responding legal entity;
- active SAM registration;
- UEI and CAGE;
- authoritative company address/website/POC;
- up to three admissible similar-contract past-performance examples, or an explicit truthful statement that fewer are available;
- evidence of credible IL5/IL6/air-gapped delivery familiarity and DoD CC SRG instructional competence;
- credible instructor staffing and Fort Hood-area delivery logistics;
- answer on ability to perform at least 50% of performance cost as prime;
- reviewed non-binding ROM assumptions;
- explicit authority for the exact email submission/public representation.

If those gates cannot be met cheaply, preserve this as acquisition intelligence and seek a subcontractor/teaming position rather than manufacturing prime capability.

## Minimum viable response thesis

Offer an operational, evidence-driven curriculum rather than generic AI literacy:

- leaders learn use-case selection, governance boundaries, failure modes, and acceptance criteria;
- practitioners learn repeatable prompting, prompt libraries, evaluation, prompt-injection defense, and data-boundary discipline;
- technical developers build governed agents using typed tools/connectors, explicit authority/custody, observability, regression tests, emergency disablement, and reproducible acceptance evidence.

The differentiator should be a demonstrable secure-agent exercise with a visible before/after evidence trail, not claims of AI expertise.

## ROM model to build once pricing authority exists

Price separately:

- per-cohort instruction for each track;
- curriculum customization;
- lab configuration/integration;
- travel/local facility logistics;
- instructor preparation/support;
- option-year escalation assumptions.

Do not quote from public course-price anchors alone. Use attributable delivery cost + required margin + risk reserve, then benchmark against comparable government/enterprise AI training only as a reasonableness check.
