Delegate any delegable duty to another officer, installing them in a bounded sub-office. Scope: any delegable duty; any officer; any sub-scope of first-officer authority.

## Limit

Cannot delegate non-delegable duties — watch management, session continuity, operational reporting to captain. Cannot transfer accountability (per Rule 2: accountability flows upward).
