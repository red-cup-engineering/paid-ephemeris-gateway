    Rules

    Five dice, up to three rolls a turn — hold whichever dice you like between rolls. Score exactly one category per turn, in any order. 12 standard Yacht categories (not 13 — that's Yahtzee's count). Game ends once every player has filled every category; the highest total wins.

    Keyboard

    1–5 toggle hold on a die · Space roll · T today's game · N new random game. Tab into the category table and press Enter/Space on a "Score" button to score that category. Every die and control is also a plain focusable button.

    Rules and odds computed live by @red-cup-engineering/parlor-kernel. Determinism: the same seed always deals the same dice.
